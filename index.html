1. Introduction

ProtonCalc is an Android calculator and unit-converter application developed by ORYNZO. It provides Basic and Scientific calculators, multiple unit converters, light and dark themes, an in-app coin balance, and optional Google or email account sign-in.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide account-related features, synchronize your data, process in-app purchases, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

The Basic and Scientific calculators operate entirely on your device and are available without an account. Opening a unit converter is free, while each successful conversion costs 12 coins. Failed conversions are not charged. New accounts start with 30 coins.

Signing in through Google or email is optional. An account is needed only for coin-based conversions, coin balance synchronization, coin purchases, and cross-device calculation history. You can use the basic calculator features without signing in.

By using ProtonCalc, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will rely on the appropriate legal basis and obtain consent when required.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you choose to use account features or when it is necessary to operate the service.

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address: Used for account identification, sign-in, and account-related communication.
  • Display name: The name associated with your Google account or provided during email sign-up.
  • Profile photo URL: The profile image URL supplied by Google sign-in or associated with your account.
  • Firebase user ID: A unique identifier used to associate your account with its balance, history, and transactions.

2.2 Coin Balance and Transactions

We store your coin balance and coin transaction records, including a transaction ID, transaction type, and amount. This information allows us to maintain your balance and record coin credits and deductions associated with supported app features.

2.3 Synchronized Calculation History

If you use an account, ProtonCalc can synchronize up to the 50 most recent calculation history entries with our server. Each entry may contain:

  • The calculation expression.
  • The calculation result.
  • The calculator mode: Basic or Scientific.
  • The angle mode: DEG or RAD.
  • The calculation timestamp.

Calculation history is used to provide cross-device access to your recent calculations. You can also maintain local history on your device.

2.4 Google Play Purchase Records

When you purchase coins, we process and store the purchase token, product ID, and number of coins associated with the purchase. Purchases require a signed-in account.

Our server verifies purchases using the Google Play Developer API before crediting coins. Purchase records help us confirm valid purchases, prevent duplicate coin credits, and detect fraudulent or abusive transactions.

We do not receive or store your payment card number, card security code, or other payment card details. Google Play handles payment processing.

2.5 Session Information

We maintain session tokens to support authenticated access to account-related services. Session tokens are stored on our server only as hashed values. Sessions expire after 30 days.

2.6 Technical Logs and Passwords

Our servers and hosting provider may process standard technical logs, such as IP address, request time, and error information, for security and troubleshooting. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password in our own database.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

ProtonCalc stores certain preferences and app data locally on your Android device. These include:

  • Your selected light or dark theme.
  • Your haptics and sound setting.
  • Your chosen profile name.
  • Your local calculation history.
  • Your sign-in session information.

Local settings and history are used to preserve your preferences and provide a convenient experience. Local data is separate from synchronized account data stored on our server.

You can clear local data through the app's Settings > Clear data / reset option. This action keeps your account and coin balance intact. Account deletion is a separate action described below.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

ProtonCalc does not collect or request access to the following categories of personal information as part of its described functionality:

  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Camera or microphone data.
  • Contacts.
  • Photos or files stored on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers.

ProtonCalc does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

We do not see or store payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

The account-related information described in this policy is collected only when you use the relevant features. The Basic and Scientific calculators perform calculations locally on your device.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information described in this policy for the following purposes:

  • Account access: To authenticate users through Google or email and associate data with the correct account.
  • Synchronization: To synchronize coin balances and recent calculation history across supported devices.
  • Purchase processing: To verify Google Play purchases and credit the appropriate number of coins.
  • Fraud prevention: To prevent duplicate purchase credits, unauthorized transactions, and abuse of the service.
  • Service security: To authenticate requests, manage sessions, and protect account-related features.
  • Support: To respond to questions, account requests, and other support communications.

We do not use the information described in this policy to serve personalized advertisements or to conduct third-party advertising tracking.

6. Third-Party Services

ProtonCalc relies on the following services to provide its account and purchase features.

6.1 Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In

We use Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In, provided by Google LLC, to support optional account authentication. Depending on the sign-in method, account information such as your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and authentication identifiers may be processed to authenticate you.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

6.2 Google Play Billing and Google Play Developer API

We use Google Play Billing to process in-app coin purchases and the Google Play Developer API to verify purchases on our server before crediting coins. Google Play handles payment processing under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

6.3 Cloud Hosting and Database

Our API server and PostgreSQL database are hosted by a cloud hosting provider. This infrastructure is used to store and process account information, coin balances, transaction records, synchronized calculation history, purchase verification records, and session data as needed to provide the service.

Information processed by these service providers is used to operate ProtonCalc and its backend features. We do not sell your personal information to these providers or authorize its use for third-party advertising.

We do not operate an advertising network or use third-party analytics or tracking services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by ProtonCalc against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Our security measures include:

  • Transmitting data between the app, server, and supported services over HTTPS.
  • Verifying Firebase ID tokens on the server before accepting authenticated requests.
  • Storing server-side session tokens as hashed values rather than as raw tokens.
  • Using secure, HttpOnly cookies for web sessions.
  • Verifying Google Play purchases on the server before crediting purchased coins.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide ProtonCalc's account features, maintain balances and transaction records, verify purchases, protect the service, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations.

  • Account information: Retained while your account remains active, unless it is deleted earlier or a lawful reason requires otherwise.
  • Calculation history: Account-synchronized history is limited to the 50 most recent entries. Local history is stored on your device until you clear it or delete the app's local data.
  • Coin records: Coin balances and transaction records are associated with your account until account deletion.
  • Session information: Server-side sessions expire after 30 days. Associated session data is deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Purchase verification records: Purchase tokens, product IDs, and coin amounts may be retained after account deletion in a form no longer linked to your account, solely to prevent duplicate crediting and fraud and to meet legal or accounting needs.

When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we will delete it or de-link it from your account, subject to applicable legal obligations and the limited purchase-record retention described above.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ProtonCalc account and associated personal data at any time using the in-app deletion option or by contacting ORYNZO by email.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open ProtonCalc on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings > Delete Account (available when you are signed in).
  3. Follow the in-app instructions to confirm account deletion.

Account deletion permanently deletes your sign-in record from Firebase Authentication and the account record from our database, including your email address, display name, profile photo URL, coin balance, synchronized calculation history, sessions, and coin transaction records. Local app data on the device is also cleared where possible.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to contact us directly, email orynzo@proton.me and request deletion of your ProtonCalc account or personal data. We may need sufficient information to identify your account and verify that the request is legitimate.

Information That May Be Retained

After account deletion, Google Play purchase verification records containing the purchase token, product ID, and number of coins may be retained in a form no longer linked to your account. Such records are retained only to prevent duplicate coin crediting and fraud and to meet applicable legal or accounting needs.

Clear History Without Deleting Your Account

If you want to clear local app data without deleting your account, go to Settings > Clear data / reset. This clears local data but keeps your account and coin balance intact. This option is different from deleting your account.

These rights may be available under privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as applicable. The availability and scope of these rights depend on your jurisdiction and the circumstances of the request.
