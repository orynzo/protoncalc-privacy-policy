1. Who we are

The data controller for ProtonCalc is ORYNZO, the developer and publisher of the App on Google Play.

Developer support contact: orynzo@proton.me

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored only on your device (no account needed)

  • Calculation history – up to the 50 most recent calculations (expression and result).
  • Preferences – theme (light/dark), touch feedback (sound & haptics) setting, and an optional display name that you choose.

This information stays on your device. Basic and Scientific calculations are performed on your device and are not sent to our servers.

2.2 Information we collect when you create an account

Creating an account is optional. You can sign up with Google or with an email address and password, which is handled through Firebase Authentication (a Google service). If you create an account, we receive and store:

DataPurpose
Email addressIdentify your account and sign you in
Display nameShow your name in the App
Profile photo URL (Google sign-in only)Show your profile picture in the App
Firebase user IDLink your account to its sign-in credentials
ProtonCoin balance and coin transaction recordsProvide the coin feature and prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions
Calculation history (up to 50 entries)Sync your history across your devices
Session token (stored in hashed form on our server) and expiry timeKeep you signed in securely; sessions last up to 30 days

If you sign up with an email address, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.

2.3 Purchase information

When you buy a ProtonCoin pack through Google Play, Google processes the payment. We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive and store only the purchase token, product ID and number of coins credited, so we can verify the purchase with Google Play and avoid crediting the same purchase twice.

2.4 Technical information

When the App contacts our servers or third-party services, your IP address and basic request information are unavoidably visible to those servers. Our server may keep limited technical logs (for example, error and security logs) for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

3. Information we do not collect

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs
  • Photos, videos, camera, microphone or files
  • Advertising ID, and no personalized advertising
  • Payment card or bank details

ProtonCalc does not contain third-party advertising SDKs. Inputs you type into the converters (for example, the BMI converter or the date/age converter) are used only to compute the result on your device and are not sent to our servers or stored in your account.

4. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store and sync your ProtonCoin balance and calculation history.
  • To verify Google Play purchases and credit coins.
  • To deduct coins when you complete a paid conversion (a successful conversion costs 12 ProtonCoins; opening a converter and using the calculator are free).
  • To protect the App against fraud, abuse and technical problems.
  • To respond to your support requests and comply with legal obligations.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the App and your account), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention and improving reliability), legal obligations, and consent where applicable.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In – account sign-in and identity verification. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play (Billing and Play services) – payments, purchase verification and app distribution. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers – to host our server and the database that stores account data.
  • Public currency-rate data sources – the currency converter fetches exchange rates from a public currency-data service delivered through content delivery networks (jsDelivr and Cloudflare Pages). These requests contain only the currency code and date requested, no account information; the networks may see your IP address.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect the safety and security of users and the App.

6. Purchases and ProtonCoins

ProtonCoins are a virtual in-app balance with no cash value. They can be used only inside ProtonCalc, cannot be transferred or exchanged for money, and are tied to your account. Purchases are made through Google Play and are subject to Google Play's terms and refund policies. If you delete your account, your unused ProtonCoin balance is permanently erased and cannot be restored.

7. Data retention

  • Device-only data stays on your device until you clear it in the App (Settings → Clear data / reset), clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Sessions expire automatically after 30 days or when you log out.
  • After you delete your account, we delete your account data as described in Section 8. Limited, de-linked purchase records (purchase token, product ID and coin amount, no longer connected to your account) may be kept to prevent fraud and duplicate crediting and to meet accounting and legal obligations.

8. Account and data deletion

Delete your account inside the App (while signed in)

  1. Open ProtonCalc and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account ("Permanently delete your account and all saved data").
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.
What is deleted: your account record (email, display name, profile photo URL, Firebase user ID), your ProtonCoin balance, your coin transaction records, your synced calculation history, and all of your active sessions. The App also deletes your Firebase Authentication user and clears the preferences and history stored on that device. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email (if you cannot sign in or no longer have the App)

Send an email to orynzo@proton.me with the subject "ProtonCalc account deletion request" from the email address registered to your account. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account. We will delete your account data within 30 days of verifying your request.

Delete data without deleting your account

You can clear your calculation history and reset your saved settings at any time using Settings → Clear data / reset. Your account and ProtonCoin balance remain intact when you do this.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), verification of sign-in tokens on our server, storing session tokens in hashed form on the server, and keeping your session credential in secure device storage on Android. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

To exercise these rights, contact us at orynzo@proton.me. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use automated decision-making that produces legal effects about you.

11. Children's privacy

ProtonCalc is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. Using the calculator and converters does not require an account. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us at orynzo@proton.me and we will delete the account and its data.
