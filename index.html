1. Introduction & Scope

ProtonCalc - Calculator (the "App") is an Android application developed by ORYNZO ("we", "us", "our"). The App provides a Basic Calculator, a Scientific Calculator, and Premium Converter Tools, and is distributed through the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes user information, what rights you may have, and how to contact us about privacy. By using the App, you acknowledge this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We distinguish between information provided through authentication, data stored locally on your device, and purchase-related information.

A. Google Authentication & Account Information

The App supports "Sign in with Google" and "Sign up with Google" through Google OAuth / Google Sign-In. For authentication, account management, and multi-device balance synchronization, the App collects only this minimal profile information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google Account ID
  • Your Google profile picture URL

This information is used strictly for the authentication, account management, and synchronization purposes stated in this Policy.

B. Calculation History & App Preferences

The App handles the following functional data:

  • Calculation history
  • Basic preferences, including Dark/Light theme settings
  • Sound and haptic feedback preferences
  • Locally stored application data and cache information

Local device storage and cache are used to keep the App working and to remember your preferences. This local data is distinct from server-side account information (Section 2A and Section 4). This Policy does not state that calculation history is uploaded to a server or synchronized across devices.

C. Purchase-Related Information

When you buy virtual coins, the App may maintain your virtual coin balance and related transaction or coin-ledger records. Payment credentials are handled by Google Play, not by us (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described in this Policy only to:

  • Authenticate users through Google Sign-In.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Synchronize virtual coin balances across supported devices.
  • Maintain purchase-related virtual coin records.
  • Enable access to premium converter tools.
  • Save calculation history and user preferences locally.
  • Maintain essential application functionality.
  • Respond to privacy-related requests and account deletion requests.

4. In-App Purchases, Virtual Coins & Google Play Billing

The App lets you purchase virtual coins through Google Play Billing (Google Play In-App Purchases).

  • Virtual coins are used within ProtonCalc - Calculator to unlock premium converter tools.
  • Google Play Billing securely handles purchase transactions.
  • ORYNZO does not directly process, collect, or store your payment card numbers, bank account details, or financial credentials.
  • The App may maintain the virtual coin balance and associated transaction or coin-ledger records needed for account management, purchase verification, and multi-device balance synchronization.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Storage

Information is held in the following places:

  • On your device: local application data, cache, calculation history, and preferences.
  • Account records: authentication-related records and account information.
  • Coin records: virtual coin balance and coin-ledger records.

Security

Data transmitted between the App and applicable backend services is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, where supported by the App's network architecture. We believe reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are important for protecting user information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Requesting account deletion starts the deletion process described in Section 6.

6. Account Deletion & Data Removal

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

A. In-App Account Deletion Path

Settings -> Privacy Policy -> "Delete Account & Data"

Users who are signed in can navigate to Settings -> Privacy Policy, where a dedicated 'Delete Account & Data' button is visible to permanently remove their account, coin balance, and associated profile data immediately.

B. Alternative Deletion Request Method

You may also request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing developer support at orynzo@proton.me.

C. Data Deleted During Account Deletion

Account deletion permanently removes the following account-associated information, subject to any legally required retention exceptions:

  • Authentication-related account records
  • Associated user profile information
  • Virtual coin balance
  • Associated virtual coin ledger records
  • Server-stored calculation history, if any
  • Other account-associated personal data covered by the deletion request

The App is designed to initiate immediate deletion of the account, coin balance, associated profile data, authentication records, coin ledger, and applicable calculation history when the in-app deletion process is completed.

Please note: Immediate deletion in the App does not mean every copy is instantly erased from every system. Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups, if applicable, and some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations.

D. Local Device Data

Calculation history, preferences, and cached information stored only on your device may need to be removed or cleared locally, as part of the deletion process or through your device's application settings, depending on the App's actual implementation. This Policy does not state that remote account deletion automatically erases all local files.
